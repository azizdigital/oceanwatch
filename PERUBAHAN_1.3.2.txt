Sentinel OceanWATCH 1.3.2
=========================
Vessels page, tidier with many vessels
- The big vessel cards on top are gone. The top now has small tabs: Around <platform>, All vessels (n), the vessel open
  now (with its job status), and Vessel settings.
- "My vessels" is a list beside the chart (below it on an iPad held upright): vessel icon, name, type, the job status
  as one word (GO / CAUTION / NO-GO), and distance / ETA when it is on the chart. Vessels on the chart come first,
  nearest first; then the old reports; then those "not on the chart yet".
- Each vessel not on the chart yet has a "MarineRadar" button (opens its MarineRadar page and the report form for it)
  and a "Report" button.
- A paste box above the chart: paste a MarineRadar / VesselFinder vessel page there and the vessel (found by its MMSI /
  IMO) goes straight onto the chart - no form. An unknown MMSI or a page without MMSI is explained.
- MarineRadar pages that only say "was last reported ... at <lat>, <lon>": speed and course are read from the page's info box
  ("5.3 kn / SPEED", "45.0° / COURSE").

Automatic positions from Open Waters AIS (openwaters.io - free for personal use, no key; data from AISHub)
- Every 15 minutes while the Vessels page is open (and on ↻), one request for all your vessels' MMSI.
- Heard in the last 6 hours: put where it was heard, then carried forward by its speed and course.
- Heard longer ago but sailing to a known place (AIS destination + ETA): estimated along the straight line from where it
  was last heard: share = (now - last heard) / (ETA - last heard). Marked "Open Waters estimate".
- Unknown destination, stale ETA (more than a day past) or no data: nothing guessed; the list says why.
- One Open Waters estimate is kept per vessel; your own reports stay. "Auto Open Waters" can be switched off.
- Checked 24 Nov 2026: in the last 90 days Open Waters heard none of these vessels within 20 nm of Irong Barat
  (shore receivers near Kemaman only), so offshore positions are estimates; a MarineRadar paste is more exact when fresh.
