Sentinel OceanWATCH 1.3.1
=========================
Vessels page
- The job status on a vessel card stays inside the card: the status first (GO / CAUTION / NO-GO), then the job name, cut
  with "..." when long (the full text shows when you point at it).
- Report a position > "From MarineRadar / VesselFinder": open the vessel's page with the MarineRadar or VesselFinder button,
  copy the whole page (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C / long-press > Select All > Copy) and paste it in the box. The vessel is picked by
  its MMSI / IMO.
  MarineRadar (free page) shows the real position: lat / lon, speed and course are used as they are; the report time is
  "now - the age of the data" ("Updated 3 hours" = 3 hours ago), then the dashed estimate carries it forward (at most
  6 hours; older data shows as an old report). Marked "MarineRadar" on the chart.
- VesselFinder (fallback): open the vessel's VesselFinder page, copy the whole page
  (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C / long-press > Select All > Copy) and paste it in the box. The free page has no coordinates, so the app
  estimates the position along the straight route from the last port to the destination:
    share of the trip = (now - departure ATD) / (ETA - departure ATD)   (VesselFinder times are UTC)
  The vessel is put at that share of the route, heading along it, at the speed VesselFinder shows; the vessel is picked
  by its MMSI / IMO. Known places: Irong Barat (the platform), Kemaman (port), the towns on the chart. Marked
  "VF estimate" on the chart, "Route estimate (VesselFinder)" in the panel. The estimate from speed never runs past the
  destination. A page with coordinates (VesselFinder Premium) is used as it is.
- The chart opens wide enough to show every reported vessel.
- Coastline: a wider area (99-109 E, 0.3-9.5 N: the Gulf of Thailand and the South China Sea islands), and the straight
  cut line at the edge of the coastline data (101°45'E) is gone.
