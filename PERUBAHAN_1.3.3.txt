Sentinel OceanWATCH 1.3.3
=========================
Fewer vessels "not on the chart yet"

Last heard position (Open Waters)
- When Open Waters heard a vessel in the last 7 days but no route estimate is possible (no destination, a destination
  the app does not know, or an ETA more than a day past), the vessel is now drawn where it was last heard, faded, with
  "last heard 2 days ago". Not carried forward (no speed / course guess). Older than 7 days: not drawn; the list says why.
- Kept 7 days; replaced on every Open Waters check. Your own reports still come first.

My places (Vessel settings)
- New section at the bottom of Vessel settings: AIS destination name -> location.
- AIS destinations the app did not know on the last check (e.g. ANGSI, RSK, SG KUANG) are offered as "+ NAME" buttons.
- "Pick platform": the 80 nearest platforms from the satellite dataset, with distance / bearing from your platform
  (orange flame = gas flare), or "own Lat / Lon" (decimal or degrees-minutes like 4°31.2'N).
- Saved places are matched first (names compared without spaces / dots), so a vessel heading to ANGSI gets an Open
  Waters route estimate toward your chosen point. A place without a valid location is refused on Save.
- Saving the settings from the area view now stays on the area view.
