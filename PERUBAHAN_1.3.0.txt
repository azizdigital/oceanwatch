Sentinel OceanWATCH 1.3.0
=========================
New look: Weather and Vessels pages
- SVG icons everywhere (platform, each vessel type, arrows, sky) instead of emoji pictures.
- Weather: the platform at the top with 4 instruments (wind, gust, wave, swell) coloured against your limits, a 72-hour
  timeline you can tap (each 6-hour period in one sentence), days 4-7, then table / charts / tide tabs.
- Vessels: a fleet strip at the top (area, all my vessels, each vessel, settings) and a stage below.
- OceanWATCH chart: the app's own nautical chart (Natural Earth coastline, no map service): the platform, 5 / 10 / 20 / 40 nm
  rings, the route from Kemaman port, wind and swell now, towns and islands; drag to move, + / - to zoom.

Position reports (vessels on our own chart)
- "Report a position": give the position the captain reports as bearing + distance from the platform OR lat / lon
  (decimal or degrees-minutes, e.g. 5°41.5'N); typing one fills the other. Or tap the place on the chart.
- Optional speed and course. Report time (default now). Note.
- On the chart: the vessel (top-down, pointing along its course), distance and ETA to the platform, its last 10 reports
  as a track. With speed and course, a dashed line shows where it should be now (estimate, at most 6 hours).
  After 6 hours the vessel fades and is marked "old report". Reports older than 3 days are dropped.
- ETA = distance / speed (straight run). Speed: the reported one, else the usual speed of the vessel's type
  (crew boat 20 kn, FCB 18, PSV 10, AHTS 11, ERRV 8, AV 7, tug 6, barge 5, tanker 10, other 10), which can be changed
  per vessel in Vessel settings. A course more than 60° off the platform's bearing says "not heading to the platform".
- One vessel: the chart, VesselFinder or MarineTraffic; the side panel shows the last report, estimate, ETA and history.

AIS (only if heard)
- With the relay set (relay v5 with the aisstream.io key in Cloudflare), the chart asks the relay every 2 minutes for AIS
  vessels within ~45 nm of the platform. Your vessels show by name, others small. Nothing heard = nothing shown.
  (Tested 24 Nov 2026: aisstream.io has no shore receiver near Irong Barat, so this is usually empty for now.)

Relay script v5 (Settings > Relay > Copy relay script; the same in all 3 apps)
- /KEY/ais: vessel positions from aisstream.io; the key is a Cloudflare secret AIS_KEY, never in the app.
